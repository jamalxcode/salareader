AI search

Search and AI topics run a small AI model in your browser (about 25 MB, downloaded once). It's free and nothing you search leaves your device.

With your own Anthropic API key, you get ✨ Summarize on articles and ✨ Brief me on any list. The key stays in this browser and is sent only to api.anthropic.com. Get one at platform.claude.com.
