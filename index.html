      AI search

      Search and AI topics run a small AI model in your browser (about 25 MB, downloaded once). It's free and nothing you search leaves your device.

      Multilingual matches by meaning across languages: an English topic also finds Arabic or Russian articles about the same thing.

      Looser → more articles, stricter → fewer, closer matches.

      AI assistant (optional)

      Pick an assistant for ✨ Summarize and ✨ Brief me: Claude, ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Grok, OpenRouter or Gemini, the same ones as jamalterm. Switch any time with Alt+1–6. Keys stay in this browser and go only to that assistant's company. Usage is billed to that company's API account (a ChatGPT Plus subscription doesn't cover it).

      Your feeds

      Most sites don't let web pages read their feeds. For those, Sala Reader asks a relay: a small server that fetches the feed and returns only feeds. Public CORS proxies are never used.

      Backup and moving devices

      Your reader lives in this browser on this device. Your phone and laptop each have their own, and clearing this browser's data erases it. To move it or keep it safe, export a backup file and import it on the other device. It holds your subscriptions, folders, read and starred articles, topics and settings.

      Privacy

      Sala Reader has no accounts and no server-side storage. Here is everything that leaves this device:

      • The shared catalog comes from this site. Everyone downloads the same files, so the site can't tell which feeds you follow or what you read.
      • Your own feeds are fetched from the feed's site, or through the relay you picked (shared by default). The relay sees only the feed's address, keeps no logs, and gets nothing that identifies you.
      • Images in articles load from their own sites when you open an article, with no referrer.
      • The AI search model is downloaded once from jsDelivr and Hugging Face. Searches run on this device.
      • ✨ Summarize and ✨ Brief me send that article or those headlines to the AI assistant you chose, only when you press them.
      • A visit counter (GoatCounter, no cookies) counts page visits. It never sees your feeds, articles or searches.
